Privacy Policy

Kawaii Photo Frame · Last updated: October 27, 2026

📄 Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Kawaii Photo Frame ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. Your privacy is our top priority.

The App is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. There are no user accounts and no registration requirements.

🛒 In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time In-App Purchase to unlock premium styles and remove restrictions. All transactions, payments, and receipt verifications are processed entirely and securely by Apple through the App Store infrastructure.

We do not collect, store, or have access to your financial information, credit card numbers, or billing details. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored at any time by tapping "Restore Purchases" in the App.

📱 Data Stored Locally

To function properly, the App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Your selected photos (temporarily saved for editing)
  • Your app preferences (e.g., visual toggle settings)
  • Your premium unlock status

This data is stored securely within the App's private sandbox. Neither we nor any third party can access this data. If you delete the App, this local data is permanently deleted.

📸 Camera and Photo Library Access

To allow you to frame your photos, the App requires access to your device's hardware and media:

  • Camera: Used only when you tap "Take Photo" to capture a new image.
  • Photo Library: Used via Apple's secure photo picker when you tap "Choose from Gallery". We only receive the specific image you select.
  • Saving Photos: When you tap "Share" or save, iOS may ask for Add Only permission to save your finished creation to your photo library.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in Settings → Kawaii Photo Frame on your iPhone.

🔗 Third-Party Services

Apart from standard App Store transaction services provided by Apple, the App does not use any third-party services that collect data. Specifically, we do not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting services
  • Cloud synchronization

🧒 Children's Privacy

The App is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, there are no privacy risks for children.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page when changes are made. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
